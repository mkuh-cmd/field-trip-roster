Field trips shouldn't feel
this stressful

Every teacher knows the anxiety. Here's what Field Trip Manager solves.

📋
Paper rosters get lost in the chaos
Wet clipboards, torn printouts, and illegible handwriting. By the time you've counted heads, the bus is ready to leave without you.
😰
No real-time visibility across buses
Chaperones are on 4 different buses and you have no idea who's been checked in until they call or text you — if they remember.
🔄
Last-minute changes break everything
A student gets added the morning of the trip. Now you're reprinting rosters, texting chaperones, and hoping nobody gets left behind.
Simple by design

Set up in minutes.
Run your trip with confidence.

Three steps and you're ready for check-in day.

Import your roster
Paste your Google Sheet URL and your student and chaperone lists import automatically. Or add names manually — takes about 2 minutes.
Generate QR cards
One click generates a QR code for every chaperone. Print them or share digitally. Each card is pre-loaded with that chaperone's exact student list.
Watch it happen live
Chaperones tap Present or Absent on their phones. Your dashboard updates instantly. You see every student across every bus in one view.
Everything you need

Built for how field trips
actually work

Every feature was designed by someone who has stood on a curb counting heads.

📱
QR codes for every chaperone
Each chaperone scans one QR code and gets their personal roster, ready to check in. No app download, no login required.
📊
Live dashboard
See every student across every bus simultaneously. Updates in real time as chaperones check in students — from any device.
📋
Google Sheets sync
Connect your existing Google Sheet and import students and chaperones in one click. No re-typing. No copy-paste errors.
🔄
Two check-in phases
Boarding check and End-of-Night check. Two separate moments of accountability so no student gets missed getting on or off the bus.
✏️
Last-minute edits
Add, remove, or rename students right up to departure. Only the affected chaperone's QR code changes — everyone else's stays the same.
🖨️
Print-ready chaperone cards
One click generates a printable PDF with every chaperone's QR code, student list, and bus assignment. Professional and practical.
