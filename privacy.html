Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Summary: We collect only what's necessary to run the app. We never sell your data or your students' data. Student information stays within your account and is never shared with third parties.

1. Who We Are

Field Trip Manager is a product of The Calculated Classroom, an educational technology company serving K–12 teachers and school administrators. You can reach us at support@thecalculatedclassroom.com.

2. What Information We Collect

Account information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from your Google account. We use this only to identify your account within the app.

Roster data: Student names, chaperone names, and bus assignments that you enter or import from Google Sheets. This data is stored in your account and is never accessed by us except to provide the service.

Check-in data: Attendance status (present/absent/boarded) recorded during events. This data is stored temporarily in our database and associated with your session.

Usage data: Basic technical information such as browser type and access times, used only for maintaining service reliability.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the Field Trip Manager service
  • To associate your trips and roster data with your account
  • To enable real-time check-in sharing between teachers and chaperones
  • To send you service-related communications if necessary

We do not use your data for advertising, analytics profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the service.

4. Student Data and FERPA

We take student privacy seriously. Field Trip Manager is designed to comply with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA).

  • Student names and attendance data are entered by and belong to the teacher/school
  • We act as a service provider and do not own or control student data
  • Student data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than providing the service to the school
  • Teachers control all student data and can delete it at any time
We recommend that teachers follow their school district's guidelines regarding what student information is appropriate to enter into third-party tools.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: We use Google Firebase to store and sync data. Firebase is governed by Google's privacy policies.
  • Google Sheets: If you connect a Google Sheet, data is read from your sheet with your permission. We do not write back to your sheet.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Google Firebase, a secure cloud database. We use Firebase Authentication to ensure only authorized users can access account data. Check-in data for active sessions is accessible to anyone with the session link, which is by design to allow chaperones to participate without logging in.

We recommend not sharing session links with people outside your trip team.

7. Data Retention

Your account data and trip history are retained as long as your account is active. You can delete individual trips from the app at any time. To request full account deletion, contact us at support@thecalculatedclassroom.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Field Trip Manager is designed for use by teachers and school staff (adults), not directly by children. Student data is entered by adult teachers, not by students themselves. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Export your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@thecalculatedclassroom.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
